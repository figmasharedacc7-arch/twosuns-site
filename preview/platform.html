The TwoSuns Platform

One enterprise environment. Shared context across growth and operations.

TwoSuns connects relevant inputs, organizational context, intelligence, people and workflows within an adaptive enterprise platform. Core and Ray provide the common foundation for every deployment. Horizon and Pulse extend that foundation into the commercial and operational capabilities required by the organization.

Core

Core: the shared enterprise foundation

Core organizes the context that gives enterprise activity meaning. It connects organizational structures, people, roles, permissions, relationships, initiatives, products, projects, assets, documents, inputs and workflows within one configurable environment.

Core also maintains the central log of platform activity, outputs and results. Work completed through Horizon, Pulse and Ray becomes available as continuing organizational context, subject to configured access and governance.

  • Organizational and operating context
  • Roles, permissions and governance
  • Input connections and information structures
  • Workflow configuration and administration
  • Activity, output and results logging
  • Knowledge continuity and traceability
Ray

Ray: context-aware support across the platform

Ray works from the context available to each user and workflow. It helps people explore information, understand relationships, create work, coordinate activity and advance configured processes across Horizon and Pulse.

  • Contextual search, explanation and synthesis
  • Role-aware assistance and briefings
  • Content and work-product development
  • Workflow and task support
  • Continuity across related platform activity

Integration

Connect with the current enterprise environment

TwoSuns can connect with current systems and information sources through APIs, configurable connectors, approved exchanges and human-supported inputs. Integration scope follows the use case, source environment, users, workflow and operating requirements.

The platform can progressively coordinate more workflows, introduce automation and consolidate selected activities as the value and fit become clear.

Deployment

Configure, deploy and expand

Implementations are developed collaboratively around the organization and selected use case. The initial deployment typically takes several weeks to three months, depending on scope, integrations and operating requirements.

Align the opportunity, expected value, stakeholders and scope
Configure context, access, workflows and platform environment
Connect relevant inputs and integrations
Deploy a focused proof of concept or enterprise implementation
Expand across additional users, workflows, integrations and regions

Decision Packs

Decision Packs can provide focused, reusable combinations of inputs, context, intelligence, workflow elements and outputs around a defined business need. These capabilities can become more deeply integrated into the enterprise platform over time.

Governance

Deployment and governance aligned with the organization

The preferred deployment is a shared managed cloud environment. Dedicated and local environments can be evaluated where operating, security, residency or performance requirements support a different arrangement.

Roles, permissions, information separation, traceability and governance are configured according to the selected environment and implementation requirements.
