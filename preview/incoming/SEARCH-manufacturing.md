# Envato searches, Manufacturing page

The six "parts of manufacturing" sections reuse the built-industry `area-*.jpg`
set, which was shot for a different taxonomy. Right now section 1 Building
Products shows a cement plant, section 3 Engineered Systems shows a conference
audience and section 6 Product Support shows commercial real estate. Construction
was fixed the same way and no longer shares these files, so replacing them here
affects nothing else.

Download from Envato Elements, rename to the filename in the heading, drop the
file in this folder, tell me, and I crop, grade and wire them in.

**Specs for all six.** Landscape, 2400px wide or more, the largest version Envato
offers, no resizing on your side. Subject on the side named below with quiet
space on the other, because a cream gradient washes across the frame and the
copy sits in the washed part. Leave headroom above heads and hard hats: a
section shows the full width of the file and trims only 33px of height, so a
subject tight against the top edge of the photo cannot be rescued in the crop.
No burned-in text, watermarks or brand logos. Warm or neutral daylight, no heavy
blue or teal grading, no night shots. Real photography over 3D renders.
Register the licence against the TwoSuns website.

## 1. `mfg-products.jpg`: Building Products and Assemblies
Products that form the structure, envelope and interior of built assets. Show
the product being made or stacked, not a building site.

```
steel framing profiles factory production line
roofing cladding panel manufacturing plant
window frame glazing factory assembly
insulation board production line warehouse
timber truss prefabrication workshop
```

Subject on the **left**.

## 2. `mfg-equipment.jpg`: Equipment, Machinery and Controls
Equipment that powers, moves, conditions and controls built assets. The subject
is the machine or the control gear, ideally with someone working on it.

```
hvac chiller plant room rooftop units
electrical switchgear panel installation technician
industrial automation control cabinet wiring
elevator machine room traction equipment
conveyor material handling system factory
```

Subject on the **right**.

## 3. `mfg-systems.jpg`: Engineered and Specialized Systems
Systems designed or configured for a specific asset. Look for the engineered
detail, a pipe manifold, a valve array, a battery rack, a fire pump set.

```
fire protection sprinkler pump room valves
water treatment plant pipes valves manifold
battery energy storage system racks
industrial pipework manifold engineered plant
facade curtain wall system fabrication detail
```

Subject on the **left**.

## 4. `mfg-offsite.jpg`: Off-Site and Industrialized Construction
Buildings and components produced in a factory and installed on site. The

```
modular building unit factory production
prefabricated bathroom pod manufacturing
precast concrete panel casting yard
volumetric module craned into position
panelized wall panel factory line
```

Subject on the **right**.

## 5. `mfg-supply.jpg`: Sourcing, Supply and Fulfillment
The flow of inputs into production and products out to customers. Racking,
picking, loading, the movement rather than the making.

```
distribution centre racking forklift aisle
warehouse order picking scanner operator
finished goods palletized loading dock
building products dealer trade counter yard
inventory warehouse wide angle stacked pallets
```

Subject on the **left**.

## 6. `mfg-support.jpg`: Product Support and Aftermarket
The service that continues after a product is installed. Commissioning, service,
spares. A technician with a tablet or meter against installed equipment.

```
service technician commissioning building equipment
field engineer tablet checking installed system
maintenance technician meter testing panel
spare parts counter technical warehouse
technical training session equipment classroom
```

Subject on the **right**.
