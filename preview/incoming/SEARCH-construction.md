# Envato searches, Construction and Infrastructure page

The six "parts of construction and infrastructure" sections currently reuse the
built-industry `area-*.jpg` set, which was shot for a different taxonomy and is
shared with the Manufacturing page. These six replace them with their own files,
so Manufacturing keeps what it has.

Download from Envato Elements, rename to the filename in the heading, drop the
file in this folder, tell me, and I resize, grade and wire them in.

**Specs for all six.** Landscape, 2400px wide or more, largest version Envato
offers, no resizing on your side. Subject on the side named below with quiet
space on the other, because a cream gradient washes across the frame and the
copy sits in the washed part. No burned-in text, watermarks or brand logos.
Warm or neutral daylight, no heavy blue or teal grading, no night shots. Real
photography over 3D renders. Register the licence against the TwoSuns website.

---

## 1. `cons-owners.jpg`: Owners, Developers and Clients
Organizations that initiate, fund and accept the work. Read the brief as the
client side rather than the building trade: people in business dress on or near
a site, or the asset they are commissioning.

```
real estate developer site visit business attire
property investor client meeting construction background
client walkthrough new building interior handover
executive reviewing development model presentation
public infrastructure authority officials site inspection
```

Subject on the **left**.

---

## 2. `cons-contractors.jpg`: General Contractors and Design-Builders
Organizations that take responsibility for delivering the work. The distinction
from section 3 is command, not labour: someone running the job, not doing a trade.

```
construction manager site office tablet drawings
general contractor supervising building site progress
site superintendent hard hat reviewing schedule
construction leadership meeting site trailer
project director overseeing structural frame build
```

Subject on the **right**.

---

## 3. `cons-trades.jpg`: Trade and Specialist Contractors
Organizations that perform defined scopes. This one wants hands on tools, a
visible trade being performed, close enough to read the craft.

```
electrician installing conduit building interior
steel erector welding structural frame daylight
mechanical pipefitter plant room installation
facade installer curtain wall glazing panel
excavator earthworks foundation trench site
```

Subject on the **left**.

---

## 4. `cons-design.jpg`: Design, Engineering and Advisory
Professionals who define what is built and how it is delivered. Studio or office,
not site. Drawings, models and screens rather than hard hats.

```
architects reviewing drawings studio daylight
structural engineer 3d model workstation screen
design team blueprints table discussion
quantity surveyor cost documents desk
engineering consultants technical review meeting
```

Subject on the **right**.

---

## 5. `cons-logistics.jpg`: Supply and Site Logistics
Networks that bring materials, equipment and labor to the work. Movement and
staging, the flow onto site rather than the building of it.

```
material delivery truck construction site unloading
tower crane lifting materials to upper floor
site laydown yard stacked materials pallets
equipment rental plant machinery fleet yard
construction site logistics gate deliveries
```

Subject on the **left**.

---

## 6. `cons-assurance.jpg`: Assurance and Approvals
Organizations that set requirements and confirm the work meets them. The visual
cue is measurement and verification: an instrument, a checklist, a sample.

```
building inspector checklist site inspection
concrete testing laboratory technician sample
land surveyor total station tripod site
safety officer briefing workers site
commissioning engineer checking building systems
```

Subject on the **right**.
